=== KEY INSIGHT ===
vLLM has two cold-start tiers, not one:
  - First-ever start: torch.compile + cudagraph capture (TTFT ~23 ms here, COLD)
  - Restart with disk cache: only cudagraph capture (TTFT 652 ms on first request, WARM1)
  - True warm: subsequent requests benefit from in-memory cudagraph (TTFT 21 ms, WARM2)
Benchmark with single request always measures worst case.
